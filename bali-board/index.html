Why vans, not Grab, for group moves
Grab caps at 6 seats and drivers can refuse large-group pickups in tourist zones — Canggu and Berawa have Grab-restricted areas near the beach clubs, so expect a 3–5 min walk to a pickup point. For anything with all 15 together, pre-book two Hiace vans with a driver through the villa manager.

Traffic windows to plan around
Thu 4–7pmWorst window of the trip. Airport road into Kuta/Seminyak is jammed. Budget 60–75 min door to door, not the usual 35–40.
Fri & Sat 5–7pmBerawa and Batu Bolong beach roads crawl as the beach clubs empty out. Either stay through dinner or leave by 4:30pm.
Sat/Sun before 9amClearest roads of the trip. Front-load anything time-sensitive — pickleball 9am, church 10am.
